# dvengine — the DeltaVerse 3D participant engine (0.0.1-alpha)

**What.** dvengine is the three.js engine the DeltaVerse enters a *cyborg space* with: a component scene graph (45 folder-is-module components), a scene **document** that is at once the scene, the room-token manifest and the ERC-721 tokenURI (`cyborg-space/1`), transform tools + inspector schemas + a framework-free studio to edit it, a `cyborg/1` network client for the participant triad, WebXR gates, and the **verse** — the refined mode where a login333 rung dresses the space, portals veil, the pieces ring shows the participant's holdings, and the cyborg aivatars breathe, walk, gesture, speak and reach along an arcball. Beside it, untouched, sits **legacy mode**: the oncyberio awe starter scene mounted through a faithful runtime reflection, so what was inherited stays visible next to what was refined.

## The posture (clean-room)

Upstream is **vendored by pinned sha** into `archive/` (`archive/SOURCES.json`: oncyberio/awe `04a07c8d…`, oncyberio/gltftransform `1026a2f7…`), **catalogued with port verdicts** in `LIBRARY.md`, and ported into local, self-contained UMD files. **Zero npm** dependencies, **no CDN**; three.js is **shared from the consumer** (`vendor/three/` is git-ignored — `node scripts/vendor-three.mjs` copies the DeltaVerse's r182 machine in). Every `.js` reads `window.THREE` at use time and passes `node --check`; the pure parts run under `node --test`.

## The two doors

- **Legacy mode** — `legacy/legacy.html`: `AweRuntime.Space(canvas).add(type, props)` over `static-scene.json`, the awe starter scene as inherited. Also `DVVerse.enter({ legacy:true })` mounts a document through `DVLegacyScene` on the real engine.
- **Verse** — `index.html` → `DVVerse.enter({ canvas, spaceId:'agora', claim, net:{url} })`: the world is built from the document (sky substrate, floor, zones, portals, the pieces ring, the aivatars, the THOT panel), the rung veils what it may not enter, the net joins the triad, the senses (when granted) drive gestures, the journey's 8 stages teleport.

## The cyborg-space/1 document — it IS the tokenURI

`DVScene.create()` → `{ $schema, format:'cyborg-space/1', id, name, concept, description, creatorId, editors[], room{roomId,contract,chain}, token{standard:'ERC-721', type:'aNFT'|'dNFT'|'iNFT'|'THOT', compatible:[ERC-7857, ERC-6551, ERC-5192], soulbound, tba}, agent{inft, thotRoot}, skin, theme, traits[], nfts[], zones[{id,name,minRole,bounds{c,r},portalTo}], agents[{id,type,name,seed,dims,say,zone}], components{id → {id,name,type,position,rotation,scale,parentId,data,collider,script}}, params, image, animation_url, legacy, createdAt }`. `validate(doc)` checks it, `canonical(doc)` gives the sorted-key bytes, `digest(doc)` their keccak256 (the on-chain `sceneHash`), `toTokenURI(doc)` a `data:application/json;base64,…` carrying `name/description/image/animation_url` — the space is the token's metadata; `fromLegacy(staticScene)` lifts an awe `SceneData` into it and records the source.

## The triad

client · host · anchor. Every participant is a **client** of the **anchor** (cyborgd, `ws://…:8790/ws`); when the anchor names one participant **host**, that participant runs the daemon's own isomorphic core (`dist/cyborgd-core.js` from cyborgd) locally, serves the others over RTCDataChannels and mirrors its snapshots back. `DVNet` / `DVPeer` / `DVHost` in `net/`; the message table in `net/README.md`.

## The aivatar

`components/aivatar` — primitive humanoid, substrate face, breath · walk · turn · gestures (greet · nod · wave · think) · speech bubbles with emotion tints · focus (click → the camera swaps to ArcballControls around it) · **the arcball arm**: the participant relative to the shoulder is the cursor on a trackball of radius = reach, the arm's yaw/pitch/extend follow the projected point, predicted locally and confirmed by the server's `snap.agents[id].arm`. Behaviour table in `components/README.md`.

## The transform tools + the asset desk

`edit/` is the awe engine-edit port (`edit/README.md`); `transform/desk.mjs` is the Node desk over the vendored glTF-Transform: `optimize · inspect · validate · bake · vrm · gates`. Gates from `transform/registry.json`:

| lane | ops | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| pure (plain JS over the vendored core) | dedup · prune · weld · unweld · quantize · dequantize · center · flatten · join · joinPrimitives · instance · resample · sparse · unlit · normals · partition · unpartition · metalRough · vertexColorSpace · sequence · clearNodeParent · clearNodeTransform · sortPrimitiveWeights · listTextureSlots · listTextureChannels · listTextureInfo · inspect | **on** |
| wasm (needs `vendor/{draco,meshopt}/*.wasm`) | draco · meshopt · reorder · simplify | off until the codecs are vendored (hooks in place) |
| native (sharp · ktx-software · mikktspace · ndarray-pixels) | textureCompress · textureResize · tangents · toktx · ktx | unavailable in the zero-dep desk |

VRM: `VRMC_vrm · VRMC_springBone · VRMC_materials_mtoon · VRMC_node_constraint · VRM` pass through read → transform → write byte-for-byte (thumbnail references nulled like upstream).

## Input: old-school joysticks, the mouse, the mic and the camera — and the field of influence

`input/` feeds ONE joystick model from every source: the keyboard sticks (**E S D F** + `A`/`C` side buttons + `Space`
thumb-fire; **I J K L ; ,** with **K the core button that only acts through combinations**; the **arrow keys**), the
**mouse** (the [ArcballControls](https://threejs.org/examples/#misc_controls_arcball) extension of the pointer onto the
ball), the **mic** and the **camera** (`DVParticipantInput`'s ladder: head → the right stick, gestures and onsets →
button pulses), and gamepads. `DVChords` turns combinations into actions. `verse/field.js` is the **field of
influence**: resizable up to the space extent − 1 (*infinity − 1*), always recognised by the DeltaVerse (degree =
r/max × the hierarchy's `outflow` dial), private · connected · open, with the **sceptre** and the **orb** as its first
items and the aivatar's arm reaching along it. See `input/README.md` and `verse/README.md`.

## Run it

```
git clone https://github.com/dddcyborgd/dvengine && cd dvengine
node scripts/vendor-three.mjs        # $DELTAVERSE/vendor/three → vendor/three/ (git-ignored), prints the revision
node scripts/serve.mjs               # http://127.0.0.1:8801/
#   /index.html            the demo: Legacy · Verse (pick a space, paste a claim, connect to cyborgd)
#   /studio/studio.html    the studio (append ?selftest=1 for a scripted pass over the command surface)
#   /legacy/legacy.html    the reflection
node scripts/check-syntax.mjs        # node --check every .js/.mjs outside archive/ vendor/ dist/
node --test test/*.test.mjs          # the pure lanes (or: npm test)
node scripts/gen-editors.mjs         # regenerate editors/ after changing a component's props
node scripts/build.mjs               # dist/: one concatenated UMD per lane + components/editors/studio/legacy/live + MANIFEST.json
```

## How the DeltaVerse consumes it

`DeltaVerse/scripts/gather-cyborg.mjs` copies `dist/` into `DeltaVerse/cyborg/dist`; the pages `cyborg/{index,legacy,verse}.html` load the lanes (`dvengine-core.js · dvengine-edit.js · dvengine-net.js · dvengine-xr.js · dvengine-verse.js`) with a plain `<script>` after the importmap module sets `window.THREE` from the DeltaVerse's own `/vendor/three`, and let `DVEngine.lazy` fetch `components/<type>/index.js` on demand. The verse registers every type it needs before building (`DVVerse.world.ensure`).

## Production deployment

Static files behind any web server (nginx, the DeltaVerse VPS tenant, IPFS): serve the repo (or `dist/`) and `/vendor/three` from the consumer. MIME: `.js`/`.mjs` → `text/javascript`, `.json`, `.glb` → `model/gltf-binary`, `.wasm` → `application/wasm`. CSP: `script-src 'self'` suffices (no inline scripts except the page's own module — hash it or move it to a file); `connect-src` must allow the anchor (`ws(s)://…:8790`) and any RPC/IPFS gateway the verse reads; `img-src`/`media-src` the asset hosts; `worker-src 'self'` for future wasm codecs. No `unsafe-eval` is needed.

## Security notes

- **No keys in the `.js` lane.** Nothing in dvengine signs: the wallet bar reads `eth_requestAccounts` + chainId only; holdings/iNFT/THOT are `view` reads.
- **Claims are parsed, not verified, client-side.** `verse/rung.js` decodes the login333 claim to dress the space; the anchor's `welcome{rung,rank}` is authoritative and `denied` is final.
- **Vouchers are redeemed by the participant's own wallet.** The client shows the typed data (`DVVerse.describeVoucher`) and never signs it.
- Object URLs from the studio's drag-drop are session-only; ship assets through the desk.
- ICE servers default to none (host candidates only); set `window.DV_ICE_SERVERS` deliberately.
